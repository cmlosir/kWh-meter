Assume that the specific heat capacity and density of the reaction mixture are the same as those of water i.e. 4.2 J g−1 K−1 and 1.0 g cm−3 respectively.

Part A: Calculate the mass of the reaction mixture and heat released

Heat released (Q) = m × c × ΔT
